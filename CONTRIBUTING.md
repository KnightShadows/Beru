# Contributing to Beru

Thank you for your interest in contributing! 

Beru is a complex orchestration engine, and we have prepared an extensive, book-length guide to help you get started.

Please see our comprehensive [Contributing Guide](docs/Contributing.md) in the official documentation for detailed instructions on:
1. Adding new libraries to the global package index.
2. Setting up your local Rust environment to hack on the core orchestrator.
3. Our strict CI/CD requirements (Formatting, Linting, and Testing).

We look forward to reviewing your Pull Requests!
